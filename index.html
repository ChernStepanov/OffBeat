            How OffBeat finds a pixel out of step

            These cases show measured NISAR HH/HV at sparse sample locations. Each GUNW result uses one actual acquisition pair; rejected samples remain unavailable. Backscatter peers are sampled locations of the same historical WorldCover 2021 class within 1 km; the band is median ± 2 × 1.4826 MAD, with at least 10 valid peers.

            GUNW is referenced to one coherent location in the same connected component. Ionospheric phase is removed; troposphere and tides remain. The reference is not proven stable. This apparent LOS change is not a subsidence rate or vertical displacement. The first epoch is zero by definition.

            No anomaly classifier or patch areas are published from this short case. Both maps use the same Esri imagery, grey under NISAR on the left and colour on the right. Imagery dates vary by location; this is geographic context, not a date-matched check. Map labels can be switched off. Missing and rejected measurements remain unavailable.
