            How OffBeat finds a pixel out of step

            These cases show measured NISAR HH/HV at sparse sample locations. Each GUNW result uses one actual acquisition pair; rejected samples remain unavailable. Backscatter peers are sampled locations of the same historical WorldCover 2021 class within 1 km; the band is median ± 2 × 1.4826 MAD, with at least 10 valid peers.

            GUNW is referenced to one coherent location in the same connected component. Ionospheric phase is removed; troposphere and tides remain. The reference is not proven stable. This apparent LOS change is not a subsidence rate or vertical displacement. The first epoch is zero by definition.

            Peer deviations and curve shapes are candidates for investigation, not confirmed events. Sparse samples do not define event areas. The left map shows NISAR samples and date-coloured coverage over a grayscale copy of the Esri context or selected Sentinel-2 image on the right. Measured HH/HV are shown in the charts. Missing and quality-rejected measurements remain unavailable.

            Welcome to OffBeat

            Radar sees through cloud and at night, but almost nobody can read it.

            OffBeat compares every NISAR sample with its neighbours of the same land cover, flags the ones that fall out of step, and lets you hear them.

            The tour takes about two minutes and uses real NISAR measurements.
