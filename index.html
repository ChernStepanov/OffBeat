      How OffBeat finds a pixel out of step

      This case shows measured NISAR HH/HV at sparse sample locations and a single 12-day GUNW pair. Backscatter peers are sampled locations of the same historical WorldCover 2021 class within 1 km; the band is median ± 2 × 1.4826 MAD, with at least 10 valid peers.

      GUNW is referenced to one coherent location in the same connected component. Ionospheric phase is removed; troposphere and tides remain. The reference is not proven stable. This apparent LOS change is not a subsidence rate or vertical displacement. The first epoch is zero by definition.

      No anomaly classifier or patch areas are published from this short case. Optical imagery is a 2020 composite for context, not a date-matched check. Missing and rejected measurements remain unavailable.
