# OffBeat: 2026 Space Apps alignment and discrepancies

Audited 15 November 2026 against repository, live site, submission draft and the six-slide draft. This is a traceable review, not a judging result or eligibility decision. The official [challenge page](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/challenges/dancing-with-the-sars/) and the 2026 [submission](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/project-submission-guide/) and [judging](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/judging-awards-guide/) guides returned 502 in the available network. The challenge wording is cross-checked against the [Athlone local-event reproduction](https://hackathlone.com/challenges/dancing-with-the-sars); the official page remains authoritative. Criteria are listed by the Space Apps [Ajmer local-event page](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/local-events/ajmer/), whose content is local-event-authored. No unsupported 2026 global weights are assumed.

| Area | Evidence and discrepancy | Action and remaining limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Relevance | The challenge asks for an interactive application using NISAR to track and visualize surface changes, selecting an area and interpreting findings. OffBeat offers real GCOV time series at eight prepared sites and exploratory comparisons; global browse coverage is not calibrated time-series data everywhere. | Describe candidate radar departures and real geographic scope; do not claim global event detection or verified causes. See [data contract](data-contract.md) and [science review](science-review.md). |
| Validity: website | The former HTML search description claimed every NISAR pixel and named clearings, floods, subsidence and earthquakes as detected events. Sparse measured locations, data gaps and unverified causes do not support that claim. | Corrected description and welcome text. The live site updates only after deployment. |
| Validity: application | Both global GIBS coverage colours and sparse peer z are exploratory; comparison is within 1 km and WorldCover 2021 classes may be stale. Amazon dense six-date analysis is bounded and retrospective; no causal event, event area or recall established. | Explain inputs and limits in [science review](science-review.md) and [Amazon external audit](amazon-external-audit-v2.md); no operational warning or global accuracy claim. |
| Presentation: draft | The old submission said three sites, OPERA catalogue overlap only, and dense windows as future work; the repo now includes eight sites, measured OPERA series and a local dense window. It listed optical counts without limiting them to the three prepared sites. | Updated [submission draft](submission-draft.md), preserving missing optical images and uncertainty. |
| Presentation: slides | The original slide 5 said OPERA measurements still needed Earthdata; slide 6 described dense/C–L analysis as future. Slide 3 used an August optical image alongside a September radar result. | [Reviewed slides](submission/offbeat-reviewed.pptx) correct these statements and flag the date mismatch; [original draft](submission/offbeat-draft.pptx) is retained for history. The 6-slide count matches a 2026 forum clarification, but the final guide remains unverified. |
| Validity: deformation | A single relative GUNW pair is not a vertical or annual subsidence rate; separate OPERA LOS and nearby GNSS are not a validated radar reference. | Revised materials preserve the LOS distinction, quality masks and reference/atmosphere caveats. |
| Creativity and presentation | Sonification and linked views make measurements explorable, but sound is an engineered mapping and has no independent event-detection validation. | Describe as exploratory interaction, not a validated or accessible-by-all detector; user and accessibility testing remain incomplete. |
| Impact | No user study, deployment outcome or operational alert performance has been measured. | State potential audiences and intended use without claiming established impact. |
| Rules and licensing | [2026 legal terms](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/legal/) require original content freely available or OSI-licensed and documentation/appropriate rights for required non-NASA materials; NASA describes Apache 2.0 as its default for original submitted content if no licence is specified. The repository has no project LICENSE; the Esri services and other assets have separate terms. | Rights holders must choose project licence and verify third-party/service terms; do not infer that all web services meet the conditions merely because they are publicly reachable. |
| Submission and acknowledgement | [2026 landing page](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/) sets Project-tab submission by 15 Nov 23:59 local; local judging can have an earlier deadline ([Islamabad example](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/local-events/islamabad/)). The registered event, final submission and team acknowledgements are not available in the repository. | Follow [checklist](submission-checklist.md), confirm registered event/time zone, actual public demo links, team review and submission.
